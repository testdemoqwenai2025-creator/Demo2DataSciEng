## 1. BIOINFORMATICS — Deeper Iterations

### 1.1 Missing Mathematical Frameworks

#### Information Theory Layer — Direct Coupling Analysis (DCA)

The most profound missing piece is Direct Coupling Analysis (Morcos et al. 2011, PLoS ONE) — the method that made AlphaFold2 possible. DCA computes the mutual information between MSA columns:

    I(i, j) = Σ_{a,b} f_{ij}(a,b) · log[ f_{ij}(a,b) / (f_i(a) · f_j(b)) ]

where f_{ij}(a,b) is the co-occurrence frequency of amino acids a at position i and b at position j in the MSA. Residue pairs with high mutual information are co-evolving — when one mutates, the other compensates. Co-evolving pairs are spatially close in 3D.

DCA was the bridge between sequence alignment and structure prediction; AlphaFold2's Evoformer is a learned version of DCA. The key insight: the attention mechanism QK^T in transformers IS a parameterised version of the DCA coupling matrix.

Suggested new page: "Co-evolution & Direct Coupling Analysis — From Mutual Information to AlphaFold"

#### Combinatorial Optimisation Layer — Pangenome Graphs

The genome assembly problem is fundamentally graph-theoretic — de Bruijn graphs for assembly (Pevzner 2001), Eulerian paths for contig generation, and the shift to pangenome graphs (vg toolkit, Hickey et al. 2023, Nature Biotechnology).

The pangenome replaces the linear reference (GRCh38) with a graph that captures all human variation simultaneously — structural variants, inversions, and complex regions become first-class citizens.

Key math: shortest path on variation graph = read alignment; min-cut on variation graph = haplotype phasing; BWT on graphs (GCSA2 index) for sublinear search.

#### Privacy Layer — Federated Learning for Genomics

Differential privacy (Dwork 2006) adds Laplace noise calibrated to sensitivity:

    f_tilde(D) = f(D) + Lap(Δf / ε)

where Δf is the global sensitivity (max change from one record) and ε is the privacy budget.

For GWAS: each SNP's β coefficient gets noise added proportional to the max effect of removing one sample. Modern: secure multi-party computation (SMPC) for federated GWAS across hospitals (Cho et al. 2023, Nature Genetics), homomorphic encryption for encrypted variant queries.

## 2. CHEMINFORMATICS — Deeper Iterations

### 2.1 Missing Mathematical Frameworks

#### Spectral Graph Theory

Molecular graphs have a graph Laplacian L = D - A (degree matrix minus adjacency). The eigenvalues of L encode molecular shape — the smallest eigenvalue is always 0 (connected), the second-smallest (Fiedler value) measures "bottleneck-ness", and the spectral gap determines diffusion properties.

Key insight: GNN message passing IS graph diffusion — h^(t+1) = σ(D^{-1}A · h^(t) · W), which is the discrete heat equation on the molecular graph.

#### Topological Data Analysis (TDA)

Persistent homology (Carlsson 2009) captures the "shape" of molecular data via Betti numbers:
- β_0 = number of connected components (fragments)
- β_1 = number of loops (rings)
- β_2 = number of voids (cages)

The persistence diagram records when topological features appear/disappear as a filtration parameter changes — a multi-scale topological fingerprint invariant to rotation, translation, and scaling.

#### Optimal Transport

The Wasserstein distance W_p(μ, ν) between two molecular electron density distributions IS the minimum "work" to transform one into the other. Sinkhorn iteration (Cuturi 2013) makes it tractable via entropic regularisation:

    W_ε(μ, ν) = min_T ⟨T, C⟩ + ε H(T)

where H(T) is the entropy of the transport plan.

## 3. MOLECULAR MODELLING — Deeper Iterations

### 3.1 Missing Mathematical Frameworks

#### Symplectic Geometry

Verlet integrator's stability comes from preserving the symplectic 2-form ω = Σ_i dp_i ∧ dq_i on phase space. This is Hamiltonian mechanics — the flow of a Hamiltonian system is a symplectomorphism.

Mathematical statement: φ*_t ω = ω where φ_t is the Hamiltonian flow. Connects to Noether's theorem — every continuous symmetry gives a conserved quantity (time → energy, space → momentum, rotation → angular momentum).

#### Representation Theory of SO(3)

E(n)-equivariant NN's foundation: irreducible representations of SO(3) labeled by angular momentum l = 0, 1, 2, ...:
- l=0: scalar (invariant)
- l=1: vector (equivariant)
- l=2: rank-2 tensor

Spherical harmonics Y_l^m(θ,φ) are the basis functions. Clebsch-Gordan coefficients C(l1 m1 l2 m2 | L M) give the coupling rules — same as quantum mechanics angular momentum coupling.

Deep insight: equivariant neural networks ARE applied quantum mechanics. MACE uses Clebsch-Gordan tensor products as its core operation — this IS angular momentum coupling, repurposed as a neural network layer.

#### Enhanced Sampling Methods

The "timescale problem" of MD — biological processes happen on μs-ms timescales, but MD can only reach μs:

1. Metadynamics (Laio & Parrinello 2003): history-dependent bias potential fills free energy wells
2. Replica Exchange MD (Sugita & Okamoto 1999): parallel tempering across temperatures
3. Markov State Models (Pande et al. 2010): spectral analysis of MD trajectories
4. Adaptive sampling: RL approach to identify undersampled states
5. Neural enhanced sampling: VAE learns slow collective variables

Key insight: MACE uses higher-order Clebsch-Gordan products (body order > 2) — captures 3-body and 4-body interactions. The body-order expansion IS the cluster expansion from condensed matter physics.

## 4. CROSS-DOMAIN DEEP INSIGHTS

### 4.1 Protein Folding IS P vs NP

Protein folding is NP-hard in general (Berger & Leighton 1998). But AlphaFold2 found a polynomial-time heuristic that works for real proteins — same situation as SAT solving (NP-complete but modern CDCL solvers solve industrial instances). Hard instances are rare in real data. Evolution selected for proteins that fold reliably — the "easy" instances.

### 4.2 Contrastive Learning IS Statistical Mechanics

Boltzmann distribution P(x) ∝ exp(-E(x)/kT) IS the softmax with temperature. Training a contrastive model IS minimising KL divergence. Free energy F = -kT log Z IS the negative log-likelihood. Statistical mechanics IS variational inference — Boltzmann was doing variational Bayes in 1877.

### 4.3 AlphaFold IS Renormalization Group

MSA → Evoformer → Structure Module IS a renormalization group flow (Wilson 1971). MSA captures long-range correlations (infrared regime). Evoformer refines to medium-range. Structure Module produces atomic resolution (ultraviolet regime). Each stage is a scale transformation.

### 4.4 Equivariance IS Quantum Mechanics

E(n)-equivariant NNs exploit representation theory of SO(3) — same math as quantum mechanics angular momentum coupling. MACE's Clebsch-Gordan tensor products IS angular momentum coupling repurposed as neural network operation. Equivariant NNs ARE applied quantum mechanics.

### 4.5 Molecular Dynamics IS Riemannian Geometry

Free energy landscape IS a function on a Riemannian manifold (configuration space). Metric tensor is the mass matrix. Geodesics are minimum-energy paths (reaction coordinates). Verlet IS the exponential map discretised to second order.

### 4.6 Optimal Transport IS Molecular Similarity

Wasserstein distance between molecular electron densities IS minimum "work" to transform one into the other. Geometry-aware metric more informative than Tanimoto. Sinkhorn algorithm IS matrix balancing — same operation as attention normalisation in transformers.
